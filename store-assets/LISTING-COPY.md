### Full description

```
eSamithi is the member app for community welfare societies (මරණාධාර හා සුභසාධක
සමිති) that keep their records on the eSamithi platform.


PURUKA (පුරුක) — YOUR SOCIETY'S OWN MARKETPLACE
අපේ දේ, අපි අතර — our things, among us.

Puruka is a small, closed marketplace shared only by the members of your own
society. Not strangers from across the country — the same people you sit beside
at meetings. That is what makes it different, and what makes it safe.

• Selling something? Post it in a minute with up to 3 photos, a price (or mark
  it negotiable), and your village or area.
• Looking for something? Post a "Wanted" listing and let the society come to
  you — a baby cot, a bicycle, a few days of labour.
• Browse by category: household goods, tools, furniture, farming equipment,
  food and produce, services, and items for rent.
• Search, or filter by price and area, to find what is near you.
• Interested? Call or WhatsApp the member directly and settle it face to face,
  the way village trade has always worked.

Puruka is completely free. There are no fees, no commission and no online
payments — eSamithi never handles your money. It simply helps neighbours find
each other, so a good cooking pot or a spare bicycle stays in the community
instead of being sold to a stranger or thrown away.

If something is not right, one tap reports a listing to your society office.


EVERYTHING ELSE IN THE APP
• Your contributions and payment history, always up to date
• Your loans, guarantees and fixed deposits, with interest and dues clear
• Receipts you can open any time — the same document the office prints
• Society notices — meetings, events and funeral announcements — with push
  notifications so you never miss one
• Your digital membership card with a QR code for meetings
• Your society's funds, shown transparently to every member
• Requests to your society (loans, certificates, information) without a trip
  to the office
• Full Sinhala and English, light and dark themes


GETTING STARTED
Ask your society for its join code. Verify yourself with your NIC and date of
birth, choose a PIN, and your membership is in your pocket. Only enrolled
members of a society that uses eSamithi can sign in.


FOR SOCIETIES
Your society's office runs the eSamithi desktop application to manage members,
finances and communication. The mobile app is free for every enrolled member.
Interested in bringing your society onto eSamithi? Write to azyntra@gmail.com.


PRIVACY
Your data belongs to your society. No ads, no analytics, no selling of data.
Privacy policy: https://api.esamithi.com/privacy
```

### Release notes / What's new (≤500)

```
Puruka now works both ways: post a "Wanted" listing for something you are
looking for, not just things you are selling. Browse Wanted and For sale
separately, and set a budget instead of a price.

You can also open the receipt for any contribution, benefit payment or loan
repayment — the same document the office prints, in Sinhala or English.
```
